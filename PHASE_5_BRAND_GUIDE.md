# Phase 5 — Brand & personality guide

## Brand idea

**NAKED CANVAS — Art After Dark** is a premium, adult-only, non-explicit fine-art and figure-study reference space. Its personality is confident and playful, never cheap or disrespectful.

## Visual system

- Background: deep charcoal `#120f11`.
- Panels: charcoal-wine `#21191c` and `#2a1f23`.
- Main text: warm ivory `#f4ece4`.
- Secondary text: muted warm grey.
- Accent: restrained wine `#8f4054` and rose `#d99ba6`.
- Supporting accent: muted gold `#c7a078`.
- Display face: Playfair Display; interface/body face: DM Sans.
- Rounded cards, subtle borders, no heavy glow or noisy gradients.

## Voice

- Default language remains English; the header switch toggles selected brand copy to Hinglish.
- Hinglish copy can be flirty, bold, teasing and lightly mischievous.
- Main navigation, age notice, rights/licence text, privacy notes and warnings stay clear and respectful.
- No slurs, degrading wording, non-consensual framing or explicit sexual copy.
- “A little bold. Fully classy.” is a tone line, not a licence to turn educational art into clickbait.

## Image direction

- Phase 5 adds 20 local ArtFlip CC0 WebP thumbnails at `assets/artflip-thumbs/`.
- All preview assets are 100×100 pixels; they are card/strip previews, not print-resolution files.
- Full-size artwork continues to open from the original museum host and requires internet access.
- Do not reuse the four Phase 2 starter-art IDs in this local preview pack.
- Do not replace the artwork metadata’s museum provenance or rights information.

## Interface rules

- Keep the primary experience readable on an Android phone.
- English/Hinglish switching is optional and stored only in browser localStorage.
- Do not add a backend, external translation service or tracking script.
- Maintain visible focus states and honour reduced-motion preferences.
